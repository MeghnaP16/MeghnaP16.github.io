About

Meghna Pandit is a PhD Scholar in the Department of Child Health Nursing at Manipal College of Nursing, MAHE, Manipal, where she has been based since November 2023. Alongside her doctoral research, she teaches theory classes and leads laboratory and clinical demonstrations for undergraduate and postgraduate nursing students. Her path into academia began in clinical practice, as a Staff Nurse in the paediatric ward, Neonatal ICU, and Mother & Baby Care unit at Spandan Hospital, West Bengal, before she returned to Manipal for postgraduate study in Child Health Nursing.
