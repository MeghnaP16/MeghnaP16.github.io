About

Meghna Pandit is a PhD Scholar in the Department of Child Health Nursing at Manipal College of Nursing, Manipal Academy of Higher Education, Manipal.

Her research brings together nursing, pediatric oncology, and oral health. Alongside her doctoral work, she contributes to undergraduate and postgraduate nursing education through theory classes, laboratory sessions, and demonstrations. Her earlier experience spans pediatric clinical nursing and nursing education.
